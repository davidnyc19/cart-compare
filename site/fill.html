Cart Compare

Set up your “Fill my cart” button

Do this once on each phone or computer. After that, one tap on the store's website puts your whole Cart Compare list into that store's cart.

Works with

Seasons (Lakewood and Toms River), Aisle 9, Nutmeg, Kosher West and Super Stop. You need to be signed in to your account on the store's website.

Which are you using?

  1. Copy the button code: Copied ✓
  2. Bookmark this page: tap the Share icon ⬆︎, then Add Bookmark. Name it Fill my cart and tap Save.
  3. Edit the bookmark: tap the Bookmarks icon 📖, tap Edit, tap Fill my cart. Clear the address underneath the name and paste the code. Tap Done.
  4. To use it: on the store's website, tap the Bookmarks icon and tap Fill my cart.
